# Neon Pelican · easter eggs (彩蛋) — SPOILERS

Seventeen small surprises, drawn in the neon HUD language of STYLE-X: glitch pops with chromatic letters, translucent HUD
cards with acid corner brackets, holo-billboards, stacked-stroke neon (no filters). Acid green is the egg accent and
every egg HUD carries the little neon egg glyph. The counter in the top-right corner ("彩蛋 EGGS x/17") stays hidden
until you find the first one (or after a minute of riding, as an empty tally). Finds are remembered in this browser
(`localStorage`, key `pb-eggs`).

Tiers: **clue** = a visible hint in the default ride (cat bubble, bottle glint, doorbell box; all findable within a
minute or two of riding by anyone who rings or clicks); **play** = found by playing with the controls and the sky;
**typed** = keyboard codes for the curious (Konami, GIMINI, BIRD, HACK, 888, BROWN); **automatic** = the odometer.
Knowledge rewards: `brown` (the pelican-on-a-bicycle benchmark), `velo` (Gimini's Velocipedia), `fortytwo`
(Hitchhiker's Guide), `gold` (Chinese lucky number 8, 恭喜发财).

The typed words and the Konami code never block the ride shortcuts. Side effects of the keys on the way are undone
when the code completes: the `A` in the Konami code and in HACK toggles the auto day cycle, the `C` in HACK switches
the camera, the `M` in GIMINI toggles sound. (The `W` in BROWN really does make the pelican wave.) (The `H` in HACK really does make the pelican hop: it jumps into the
hack.)

Only the viewer discovers eggs. The bell, hop and gulp detectors ignore every rig beat with `auto: true` (the
director's encounter beats and the beat keeper's fillers), and the rider's own reaction beat after a find (a bell or a
wave) is flagged so it cannot chain into another egg. Left alone the ride unlocks nothing except the odometer eggs
(`km` after about four minutes, `fortytwo`), which are rewards for watching.

## Performance notes
- No SVG filter anywhere in the eggs: glows are stacked strokes and static gradients; flicker is opacity/visibility.
- HACK flips one class on `#scene` going in and one going out (a single repaint of the static sheets each time,
  hidden inside the glitch); in between nothing repaints that would not repaint anyway. The glitch jumps are the CSS
  `translate` property on whole composited sheets (compositor only). The rider slots, front fx and HUD are excluded.
- Every egg is built once; nothing is created at run time.

## For developers and tests
- Code: `src/fx/eggs.js` (art, X-sheets, detectors), the mech suit `src/fx/mech.js` (plates hung inside the rider's joint slots via
  scene.js `overlay`, so they ride every joint; review shots: `node tools/mech-film.mjs [--extra]` -> `shots/mech/`), glyph outlines `src/fx/egg-glyphs.js` (generated by
  `src/fx/egg-glyphs.gen.mjs` from DejaVu Sans Bold, DejaVu Sans Mono Bold and WenQuanYi Zen Hei; the generator scans
  eggs.js for every CJK character), counter in `src/ui/ui.js`.
- Every egg is a start time plus a pure function of sim time, distance and pose, so `window.__pb.renderAt(t)` is exact.
  Street props follow pure schedules: `benchAt(d)`, `signAt(d)`, `puddleAt(d)`, `bottleAt(d, t)`, `shootAt(t)`.
- Screen cards re-anchor on portrait phones (centred under the top HUD, shrunk to fit); pops are clamped into view.
- `window.__pb.eggs` = `{ list, trigger(id), found(), stopAll(), reset(), setMech(on, stop), mech(), setBrown(on), setGold(on), signAt, benchAt,
  shootAt }`. The UI listens to the bus event `egg:found { id, zh, en, count, total }`.
- `node tools/check-eggs.mjs --sheet` triggers each egg, shoots it into `shots/eggs/egg-*.png` (plus `eggs-sheet.png`),
  then drives the live detectors (Konami, GIMINI, BIRD, HACK, 888, clicks on the sun / moon / bottle / a meteor, bell
  spam, a bell at the NO PELICANS sign, a gulp at night) and checks that no side effect is left (day cycle, sound,
  camera, wireframe class, jittered sheets) and that there are no console errors. Last, it fast-forwards 60 s of idle
  live play at night (`__pb.sim`) plus an injected auto gulp and auto bell spam, and fails if any egg is found.
